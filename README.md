# TLOPS

we are the founders of TLOPS and we are developing a tinkering lab online booking system. It is a centralized inventory management system specially for the tinkering labs of colleges and universities.



One day we went to tinkering lab of our university to get some materials for our project and we saw the teacher struggling to manage and allot materials as there were multiple students who were also there to obtain materials for their project. So when we got an opportunity to develop a website for a subject in our course then we thought it would be a great problem statement to work on and we started working on it.
